# PRism Brand Guidelines

## 1. The Mark
The PRism mark is a minimal geometric construction that represents code structure intersecting with AI analysis. The isometric cube grid implies logic and branches, while the central 'eye' conveys review and intelligence.

## 2. Clear Space & Minimum Size
- **Clear space:** Leave a minimum clear space equal to the diameter of the central eye around the logo at all times.
- **Minimum size:**
  - Print: 10mm wide.
  - Digital: 16px wide (for the symbol), 64px wide (for the horizontal lockup).

## 3. Brand Colours
- **Primary Violet:** `#7C3AED` (RGB: 124, 58, 237)
- **Black:** `#000000` (RGB: 0, 0, 0)
- **White:** `#FFFFFF` (RGB: 255, 255, 255)

The logo works strictly in one color, adapting effortlessly to Dark and Light modes.

## 4. Usage Rules
- Do not alter the stroke width or geometry.
- Do not add gradients, drop shadows, or glow effects.
- Do not mix bold and regular weights in the wordmark.
- Do not shrink the gap between the symbol and the wordmark.

## 5. Typography
- Wordmark uses a system-UI sans-serif at bold weight (700) with `-0.02em` tracking.
- Do not substitute the font family with heavily stylized or serif fonts.
